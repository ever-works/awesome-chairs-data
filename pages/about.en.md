# About Awesome Chairs

Awesome Chairs is a curated, continuously updated directory of computer and gaming chairs for people who spend their day sitting — developers, gamers, designers, streamers and anyone working from a desk.

## What you'll find here

- **Office and ergonomic chairs** — mesh, executive, task and reclining chairs built for long sessions.
- **Gaming chairs** — budget, premium and esports models.
- **Specialized seating** — big & tall chairs, standing and active seating, kneeling chairs.
- **Accessories** — cushions, footrests, casters and everything that makes a chair more comfortable.

## How the directory is maintained

The directory is built and maintained with the [Ever Works](https://ever.works) platform. AI agents research new products, keep existing listings current and organize everything into categories and tags; every listing links back to its original source so you can check current pricing and availability.

Missing a chair that deserves a spot? Use the **Submit** page to suggest it.
